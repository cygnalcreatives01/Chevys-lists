CHEVY'S LISTS - IPHONE VERSION (version 1.16.0)

iPhone cannot install APK files. Instead the app installs from a website, and then looks and works like a normal app on your home screen. No computer needed.

STEP 1: Put the app on a free website (one time)
1. On your iPhone, open the Files app, find this zip, tap it to unzip.
2. In Safari go to github.com and make a free account.
3. Tap the aA button > Request Desktop Website.
4. Create a new Public repository named chevys.
5. Tap "uploading an existing file", choose ALL the files from the unzipped folder, then Commit changes.
6. Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > / (root) > Save.
7. After about 1 minute your link is  yourname.github.io/chevys

STEP 2: Install it
1. Open that link in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow) at the bottom.
3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
4. Open Chevy's Lists from your home screen.

Notes: the timer sound starts after your first tap in the app. Keep the app open while the timer runs. Use Safari for installing (iOS 16.4 or later also allows Chrome).
